## 1 Scope and method

1. **DAG.** Re-parsed `planning/issue-breakdown.md` with an independent script (regex over `#### MERC-…` blocks; `Blocked by`, `P50/P90`, `Env`, `Security label`, `*(conditional)*`). It does not use `devorch.py`. Computed counts, unknown and base→conditional edges, cycles (DFS), the longest P50 path, total float per issue, transitive prerequisite and downstream counts. Compared field by field with `data/dag.json` and with every column of the 05 §5 table. Also regenerated `dag.json` with `devorch.py dag` into a scratch `DEVORCH_HOME` and diffed it.
2. **Schedule model.** Read `schedule_model.py` line by line. Re-ran it, both default and `--plan`, and diffed the output against 05 §4 and every "Model start → finish" cell in 05 §5. Then ran an instrumented copy in the scratchpad that logs Mac/operator-lane occupancy and queueing, plus a corrected-priority variant, and tried to reproduce the sensitivity table.
3. **05 §3.** Checked each of the 10 answers against the DAG and the gate definitions in threat-model §8.10 and roadmap §1.
4. **Waves and assignments.** Checked them against `assignments.json`, 06 §4 (path ownership), 09 §2 (WIP limits) and 10 §1 (integration batching).
5. **12 §2–§3.** Checked RA-D1…D6 and DOD-01…08 against the DAG, MERC-030's scope and OD-01…37 in traceability §9.
6. **Phase 0 and human dependency.** Checked operator-lane modelling against roadmap §6 (calendar, ASM-F-10), OD-16 and RISK-41/46.

## 2 Verdict

**Ready with conditions.**

The dependency analysis is correct and reproducible. Every DAG number in 05 matches an independent parse exactly. The model does not divide effort by agent count, and estimates are clearly labelled as such.

The **schedule numbers in 05 §4–§6 are not yet fit to plan against**, for four reasons:
- a prioritisation bug on the binding resource (the operator/Mac lane);
- no operator PR-review/merge load;
- an M9 calendar that contradicts the approved roadmap and OD-16;
- an operator-availability assumption that contradicts RA-D4.

**Conditions:** fix R3-01…R3-04 and re-run the model, make the sensitivity table reproducible (R3-05), and resolve the two gate-ordering items (R3-06, R3-07). No finding affects human merge authority or the production security model, so none is Critical.
