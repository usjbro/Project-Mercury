## 2 Verdict

**Not ready as-is → Ready with conditions once C-01 and H-01…H-08 are fixed.**

The separation of development from production is sound. S1–S4 agree with SEC-204, SEC-209 and SEC-310. No finding gives an agent merge or approval authority, and nothing weakens the production security model. The TDD gate content is mapped correctly to G-S1…G-S5.

However, the executable state machine in `devorch.py` cannot carry several task classes the package depends on:
- operator and Mac tasks;
- interface freezes;
- review and integration subtasks;
- failed-task recovery.

The `ledger/` layout also breaks `verify-ledger` at the first escalation. Two design-level conflicts with approved planning artefacts need fixes and an operator decision:
- the DoD requires updates to the docs and the requirements traceability matrix (RTM) that the package forbids;
- the roadmap's P0d/RISK-45 own-repo governance.

Counts: **Critical 1, High 8, Medium 12, Low 8.**
